Northstar Labs — People Handbook, effective 1 January 2026. This is a fictional demonstration company.
Annual leave: Full-time employees receive 24 paid vacation days per calendar year. Up to 6 unused days carry into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Contractors do not receive paid vacation through this policy. Leave requests go to the employee manager at least 10 business days before departure. Medical leave is covered by a separate policy; this document does not specify its allowance.
Remote work: Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week. International remote work requires advance approval from People Operations. The handbook does not name individual managers or list salaries.
