# Folio demo — fictional Northstar Labs

These files contain invented company information. Use a dedicated demo account, never personal or confidential material.

1. Create a collection called **Northstar Labs** and upload the five text files in this folder. Wait until they show Ready to explore.
2. Ask **Who owns Atlas, and what is its launch date?** Open the cited passage. Expected: Mira Chen; 18 November 2026. The security review remains a launch dependency.
3. Ask **What is the meal reimbursement limit for trips in 2026?** Expected: $55 per day, from the current policy. The archived 2025 allowance is $35.
4. Ask **Compare the meal reimbursement limit and receipt deadline in 2025 and 2026.** Expected: $35 / 14 days versus $55 / 30 days, with both sources cited.
5. Select the 2026 expense document and ask **What is the hotel nightly limit in 2026?** The correct response says that the document does not specify it.
6. Name and save the conversation, reload, open Saved conversations, then export the answer and sources.

Local AI may take time to warm up. Show the original passages while explaining that citations are evidence to inspect, not a guarantee that an answer is correct. These demonstration questions are also evaluation fixtures, not an independent quality benchmark. Results and limitations are recorded in evaluation/ANSWER_QUALITY.md.
